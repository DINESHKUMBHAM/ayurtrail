About Us

AIIA Clinical Trial Management System

A Good Clinical Practice (GCP) compliant platform for conducting and monitoring Ayurvedic clinical trials — from participant onboarding and double-blind randomization through electronic case report forms, pharmacovigilance and regulatory export.

The system captures Ayurveda-specific clinical data alongside conventional trial data: baseline Prakriti assessment, observed Dosha imbalance and Anupana details are recorded as first-class fields on every visit record.

Regulatory Scope

  • 21 CFR Part 11 — electronic signatures with signer re-authentication, SHA-256 record hashing, and tamper detection on verification.
  • Record immutability — once an eCRF is signed it is permanently locked against modification.
  • Audit trail — every action is appended to an immutable log, retained even when the originating transaction is rolled back.
  • Double-blind integrity — treatment allocation is masked in the query layer, so blinded roles never receive the real arm.
